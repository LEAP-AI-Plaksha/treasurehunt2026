Hand tracking and the 7 quick gestures run on Google's pretrained MediaPipe Gesture Recognizer (on-device, no upload). Fingerspelling letters are matched with a hand-written geometric classifier layered on the same hand landmarks, not a trained model — treat borderline calls (M/N/T/S, U/H, V/K/R) as approximate and test your lighting/framing before competition day.
